\item Which of the following statements about \textbf{meiosis} is correct?

\begin{tasks}(1)
    \task Meiosis produces two diploid daughter cells identical to the parent cell.
    \task Meiosis I separates homologous chromosomes, reducing the chromosome number by half. \ans
    \task DNA replication occurs between meiosis I and meiosis II.
    \task Crossing over occurs during metaphase I of meiosis.
\end{tasks}

\begin{solution}
\begin{align*}
\intertext{Meiosis is a type of cell division that produces haploid gametes from diploid cells.}
\intertext{Option (a): Incorrect — meiosis produces four haploid cells, not two diploid cells. Mitosis produces two diploid daughter cells.}
\intertext{Option (b): Correct — in meiosis I, homologous chromosomes are separated, halving the chromosome number from $2n$ to $n$.}
\intertext{Option (c): Incorrect — DNA replication (S phase) occurs before meiosis I begins, not between meiosis I and II.}
\intertext{Option (d): Incorrect — crossing over (chiasmata formation) occurs during prophase I, not metaphase I.}
\end{align*}

Therefore, the correct option is (b).
\end{solution}
